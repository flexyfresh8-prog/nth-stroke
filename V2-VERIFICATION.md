# V2 verification

## Reproduction

```sh
npm test
npm run build:export
npx tsx scripts/verify-v2.ts /tmp/nth-v2-renders astronaut.png manga-reference.jpeg 6000
```

The eight-fixture comparison used a seed of 20260101, a requested maximum of 6,000 strokes per run, fidelity and detail set to 1.0, and local models and timed limits disabled. V1 was stepped at a deterministic evaluation cadence and kept its original early-stop behavior. V2 and V1 therefore share the budget ceiling, with realized counts shown below. Both were scored against the same white-composited reference luminance grid at each fixture's working resolution. The accuracy series measures actual rendered pigment at intervals. Processing time covers engine computation and excludes simulated pen travel and benchmark metric sampling.

- The shaded sphere and landscape use existing procedural fixtures.
- The cube, anime illustration, rosette line art and high-contrast graphic are generated by the verification script.
- The real portrait is the public-domain NASA image of Eileen Collins distributed with [scikit-image](https://scikit-image.org/docs/stable/api/skimage.data.html#skimage.data.astronaut).
- The manga reference is the original glasses portrait supplied by the user. The report publishes its scores. The image and its renders are supplied separately with the verification bundle.

The glasses portrait extended to 24,000 strokes reached **0.746 SSIM**, **0.915 MS-SSIM** and **0.058 luminance MAE**. The eye contours, pupils, glasses bridge, nose and mouth were recognizable in the 6,000-stroke render. The extended run reduced uneven saturation in the hair and tightened thin contour breaks. The fine hair texture and shaded facial gradients remain less precise than the reference.

The sphere preserved the highlight and cast-shadow relationship. The cube retained its faces and edge intersections. The real portrait recovered the face and helmet while retaining visible texture in complex shadows. The anime example retained the figure and skyline with less fine linework. The landscape rendered the major tonal planes. The line-art fixture preserved most arcs. Solid black regions in the high-contrast graphic still showed pencil texture.

## Automated coverage

- The 47-test suite checks both engines. V2 tests cover a blank start, monotonic improvement over the sphere run, lossless pigment checkpoints, deterministic results across stepping rates, replay, continuation after completion, and redistribution mass.
- The static export builds with TypeScript checks enabled. It is served under the GitHub Pages repository prefix, including the worker and model assets.
- Headless Chromium tests exercised Dark, Light and System, engine switching during generation, pause and cancel, project reopening, PNG export, diagnostics, comparison, and the fallback canvas transport. The local Face Landmarker loaded from the static site and classified the real portrait. Tablet and phone checks measured the responsive layout. iPad Safari hardware remains unverified.

All scores are measurements on these fixtures. Pencil texture, imperfect facial detail and a 384-pixel maximum standard grid limit accuracy on highly detailed inputs.
